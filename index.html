    Help & Frequently Asked Questions

    Use this guide to get started and learn how the task manager works. Scroll down to explore each question.

    How do I add a task?

    Enter a task in the text field and press Add Task. Blank tasks show an error.

    How do I mark a task complete?

    Click the checkbox beside a task. Click again to mark it incomplete.

    How do I delete a task?

    Click the Delete button next to the task you want to remove.

    How do I clear completed tasks?

    Press Clear Completed to remove every checked task.

    Will my tasks be saved?

    Yes. Tasks are saved in your browser's local storage on this device.

    What if I submit an empty task?

    An error message appears asking you to enter a task.

    Can I use this on my phone?

    Yes. The layout adjusts to smaller screens.

    How can I stay productive?

    Start with one important task, split large projects into smaller steps, and check off tasks as you finish them.
